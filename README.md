# Growwa

Growwa is an Android launcher. It takes the place of the system home screen and keeps the wallpaper in view, with a simple grid, a frosted dock, and room for widgets.

## Home

Apps, folders, and widgets sit on the home screen. The world clock shows a few cities at once, and you can add or remove cities after the widget is already placed. Long press a widget to drag it to another spot, pull an edge to change its size, then tap the wallpaper to set it down.

Long press the wallpaper to edit the layout. Empty spots are outlined, pages can be moved, and shortcuts open wallpapers, widgets, or settings.

## Dock

The dock is the frosted tray along the bottom. It can shrink as more apps are added, or you can turn that off and pick one height. You can also set how round the corners are and how clear the tray looks.

## App drawer

Swipe up to see every app. The wallpaper blurs behind the list. Apps you open most often appear as suggestions, and you choose how many of those to show. Long press an app for its shortcuts, along with uninstall, app info, and add to dock. Drag an app out of the drawer and empty cells on the home screen show where it will land. An X cancels the drop.

## Finder

Swipe down to search. Finder looks through apps, contacts, settings, and files, then opens the match you pick. Suggestions stay hidden while you type.

## Sidebar

A short handle rests on the right edge until you drag it open. The panel lists apps you pin in settings, and you can slide the handle up or down so it sits where your thumb reaches. Open, it leaves a strip of wallpaper between the panel and the edge of the screen.

## Gestures and private space

Swipe up opens the app drawer. Swipe down opens Finder. A double tap can lock the screen. Opening the notification shade or locking the screen from a gesture needs the accessibility service.

On newer versions of Android, a private space can sit at the top or the bottom of the drawer, or stay hidden.

## Settings

You can change the home grid and the drawer grid, icon size, label size, and whether labels show. Icon packs, theme, and accent color are there too, along with dock transparency and folders that Finder should skip.

When you want the home gesture to open Growwa, set it as the default home app in system settings.
